# Skit shots: the image prompts

Every shot is generated from the same two character design sheets (cartoon Jason, made from one photo of him, and Claude as a tiny retro computer whose screen face is the Claude logo), plus the first wide shot as a set reference, so both characters and the room look the same in every shot. The voices come from one multi-speaker Drama 3 generation (`02-skit.txt`); each shot's line is then lip-synced onto its keyframe.

**Style line added to every shot:** 3D animated feature-film style, same characters as the reference sheets, same room, late-night desk lamp and monitor glow, cinematic depth of field, 16:9, no text.

**Character sheets**
- Cartoon Jason: "Character design sheet of this exact man as a 3D animated feature-film cartoon character: keep his face shape, eyes, eyebrows, messy dark swept hair and plain black t-shirt so he is clearly recognizable, with slightly stylized proportions. Front and three-quarter view, mouth closed, plain light gray background."
- Claude (references: the first Claude sheet plus the Claude logo): "Redesign this retro beige CRT computer so its screen face is the orange Claude starburst logo: the starburst fills the glowing screen in the logo's exact terracotta orange, with two simple expressive cartoon eyes and a small mouth on its center. Same beige CRT body, keyboard and coiled cable. Front and three-quarter view, plain light gray background."
